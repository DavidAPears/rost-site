1. Introduction

RÖST is committed to providing a safe environment for adults to discover voices, connect with other people and create audio content.

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation, trafficking and any attempt to use our platform to harm children.

RÖST is intended exclusively for people aged 18 and over. Children and minors are not permitted to create accounts or use the platform.

This policy explains our child safety standards, prohibited activities, reporting procedures and the actions we may take when child safety concerns arise.

2. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to all users, accounts, content and interactions across RÖST, including:

  • Discover: Public voice recordings, topics, conversations, community spaces and other user-generated content.
  • Connect: Voice-first dating profiles, voice answers, matches, private voice messages, photo reveals and any supported in-app calling features.
  • Create: Creator profiles, published recordings, personalised audio requests and communications between creators and their audiences.
  • Other interactions: Profile information, photographs, usernames, descriptions, links and any other content or communication made available through RÖST.

Our child safety rules apply to both public and private activity on the platform.

3. Age Restrictions

RÖST is an adults-only platform.

Users must be at least 18 years old to register, maintain an account or participate in any RÖST feature.

Users must provide accurate information about their age and must not misrepresent their identity or age to gain access.

Where we have reason to believe an account belongs to someone under 18, we may restrict or suspend access while we investigate and take appropriate action.

We may require additional age or identity verification where necessary and proportionate.

Our age restrictions apply to all areas of RÖST, including Discover, Connect and Create.

4. Definitions

Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE): Any conduct, communication or material involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of a person under 18, including grooming, coercion, trafficking, sexual solicitation and other exploitative activities.

Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Images, videos or other material depicting or otherwise constituting child sexual abuse material under applicable law.

Grooming: Building a relationship or establishing trust with a child for the purpose of sexual abuse, exploitation or other harm, including through online communication.

Minor: Any person under the age of 18.

5. Prohibited Activities

RÖST strictly prohibits:

  • Uploading, sharing, requesting, distributing or promoting CSAM.
  • Using voice recordings, messages, photographs, profiles or other features to groom, solicit, exploit or sexually abuse minors.
  • Attempting to contact or establish inappropriate relationships with minors.
  • Impersonating a minor or misrepresenting an individual's age for exploitative purposes.
  • Encouraging, arranging, facilitating or promoting child sexual exploitation or trafficking.
  • Sharing links, instructions or information intended to facilitate access to CSAM.
  • Threatening, coercing, blackmailing or exploiting another person using sexual content involving minors.
  • Using creator requests, private messaging, dating features or audio calls to facilitate child exploitation.
  • Attempting to circumvent age restrictions, account suspensions or safety measures.

These prohibitions apply regardless of whether the conduct occurs publicly, privately or through an attempt to move a conversation to another service.

6. Prevention and Safety Measures

RÖST takes child safety seriously and aims to maintain proportionate safeguards appropriate to the platform's features and risks.

Our approach includes:

Adults-only access

RÖST restricts registration and participation to users aged 18 and over. We may investigate accounts suspected of belonging to minors or misrepresenting their age.

Reporting and blocking

Users can report suspected abuse or inappropriate behaviour and block other users through the safety controls provided in the app.

Content and account moderation

We may review reported content, accounts and interactions to assess suspected violations of this policy and our Terms of Use.

Voice and private communication safety

RÖST recognises that voice messages, dating interactions and creator requests can present safety risks. Our safety standards apply to these features, including any attempts to use voice recordings or private communication to groom or exploit minors.

Enforcement

We may remove content, restrict functionality, suspend accounts or permanently terminate access where child safety violations are identified.

Ongoing improvements

We will review and develop our safety processes as the platform evolves, including assessing the risks associated with new communication and creator features.

7. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

We encourage users to report suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming or other child safety concerns immediately.

Reports can be submitted through:

  • In-app reporting: Use the Report feature associated with a profile, recording, message or other relevant content, where available.
  • Email: safety@rost.fyi

Please provide as much relevant information as possible, such as the account name, description of the concern and where the activity occurred.

Do not download, copy, forward or redistribute suspected child sexual abuse material when making a report.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police or relevant emergency services.

In the United Kingdom, suspected online child sexual exploitation can also be reported to the National Crime Agency's Child Exploitation and Online Protection service (CEOP).

8. How We Handle Reports

When RÖST receives a child safety report, we will assess the information and determine appropriate action based on the nature and seriousness of the concern.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

  • Reviewing the reported account, content or interaction.
  • Restricting access to relevant content or platform features.
  • Suspending or permanently terminating an account.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally required or appropriate for an investigation.
  • Reporting suspected offences to relevant law enforcement agencies or child protection organisations where required by law or otherwise legally appropriate.
  • Cooperating with lawful requests from relevant authorities.

We aim to prioritise reports involving potential harm to children.

We do not guarantee that every report will result in account termination or that we can disclose the outcome of every investigation.

9. Enforcement and Consequences

Violations of this policy may result in immediate action, including permanent account termination.

Serious suspected offences may be referred to appropriate law enforcement or child protection authorities.

Users whose accounts are terminated for child sexual abuse or exploitation are prohibited from creating replacement accounts.

RÖST may also take action against users who knowingly facilitate prohibited activity or attempt to evade enforcement measures.

10. User Responsibilities

All RÖST users are expected to contribute to a safe environment.

Users must:

  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Follow RÖST's Terms of Use and community safety standards.
  • Respect other users' privacy and boundaries.
  • Report suspected child sexual exploitation or grooming.
  • Refrain from sharing illegal or exploitative material.
  • Never use RÖST to solicit, contact or exploit minors.

Users should not attempt to investigate suspected child exploitation themselves or confront suspected offenders where doing so could create additional risk.

11. Privacy and Confidentiality

Child safety reports and related information will be handled in accordance with RÖST's Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.

Information may be shared with law enforcement, child protection organisations or other relevant authorities where required or permitted by law.

We aim to limit access to sensitive report information to those who need it for safety, investigation, compliance or legal purposes.

12. Compliance and Policy Review

RÖST is committed to complying with applicable child protection and online safety laws and relevant app store child safety requirements, including Google's policies concerning child sexual abuse and exploitation.

We will periodically review this policy and our associated safety procedures to reflect changes in legislation, platform functionality and identified risks.

This policy should be read alongside RÖST's Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and other applicable safety standards.
