RÖST is a voice-first social platform. You can share short voice recordings through Discover, meet people through Connect, and offer or request voice recordings through Create. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Our approach: Your Discover identity and your Connect dating identity are intended to be separate. A photograph you provide for Connect is not displayed to other Connect users unless both people agree to reveal their photographs through the app's reveal process. However, voices can be identifying, and no technical measure can prevent another person from recording or sharing something they hear using a separate device.

3. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Account and identity information: email address, account identifier, date of birth or age confirmation, usernames, display names, authentication information and settings.
  • Public Discover information: voice recordings, captions or descriptions, topics, tags, reactions, reports, and any profile details you choose to make public.
  • Private Connect information: your Connect name, answers to voice prompts, dating preferences and profile information, concealed profile photographs, likes, matches, voice messages, mutual reveal choices, blocks and reports. Your Connect information is intended to be separate from your public Discover profile.
  • Create information: your creator profile, descriptions of services or recordings you offer, voice samples, requests, messages and, if enabled, information needed to arrange payments or payouts.
  • Communications: messages you send to us, support requests, feedback and communications with other users through the app.
  • Technical and usage information: IP address, device and operating-system information, app version, approximate location inferred from IP address, log events, feature usage, crash reports and security events.
  • Permissions and optional information: microphone access when you record, photo-library or camera access when you choose to upload or capture a photo, notification tokens if you enable push notifications, and any optional location information you choose to provide.
  • Website information: email addresses submitted to the launch notification list and information collected by essential cookies or similar technologies. Optional analytics or marketing technologies, if used, are described in Section 10.

Voice recordings can contain sensitive information, even when you do not intend to share it. Please avoid including private information about yourself or others in public recordings.

We do not describe ordinary audio files as biometric voiceprints merely because they contain a voice. If we later use voice recognition to uniquely identify or verify people, we will explain that processing separately and establish the required legal basis and safeguards before introducing it.

4. What other people can see

Discover

Content you choose to publish to Discover may be available to other RÖST users and, CONFIRM: if applicable, to people outside the app. Public recordings can be heard, repeated, described or captured by others. Your public profile may show the details you choose to make visible.

Connect

Connect is designed for voice-first dating. People browsing Connect can hear the voice answers you make available for your Connect profile, and can see the non-photo profile information you choose to display. Your Connect photograph is concealed at this stage.

When two users match, they can exchange voice messages. The product is designed to offer a photograph reveal only after each person has sent three voice messages, and only if both people agree. If either person does not agree, the photographs should remain concealed in that conversation.

A match, a like, or sending voice messages does not by itself grant permission to reveal a photograph. Connect details and activity are not intended to appear on your public Discover profile.

Important: A blurred or colour-derived visual may still disclose general colour information about a photograph. The app should not deliver an accessible original photograph to the other user's device before mutual consent. These protections must be verified in the live implementation.

Create

Information you publish on a creator page, including voice samples and descriptions of your services, may be visible to people browsing Create. Requests and private communications are visible to their intended participants and to authorised personnel when needed for support, safety or legal reasons.
