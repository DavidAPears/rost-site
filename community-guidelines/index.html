Be yourself. Respect everyone else.

RÖST is a place for real voices, interesting people and conversations worth having.

Tell a story. Share an opinion. Make someone laugh. Read a poem. Meet someone new. Or put your voice to work.

We want RÖST to be a place where people feel comfortable speaking their minds, expressing themselves and discovering something unexpected.

We're not asking everyone to agree. We're just asking everyone to be decent to each other.

1. Keep it real

Your voice is what makes RÖST interesting.

Speak naturally, be yourself and share things that mean something to you. You don't need a professional microphone, a perfect accent or a polished performance.

A funny story recorded in your kitchen can be just as interesting as a professionally produced podcast.

Originality, personality and authenticity matter more than perfection.

2. Be a decent human

It's pretty simple: treat other people the way you'd like to be treated.

  • Be respectful, even when you disagree.
  • Debate ideas without attacking people.
  • Don't bully, threaten, harass or intimidate others.
  • Don't use hateful or discriminatory language directed at people because of who they are.
  • Respect people's boundaries, privacy and choices.
  • Don't spam, scam or deliberately mislead people.

Strong opinions are welcome. Abuse isn't.

And remember, there's a real person on the other end of every recording.

3. Think before you record

RÖST is a platform for sharing voices, so think about what you're putting out into the world.

We encourage humour, creativity, debate, storytelling and honest conversations. But please avoid content that is gratuitously offensive, threatening, sexually explicit or designed to humiliate or harm others.

Some subjects are naturally difficult, controversial or emotional. That's fine. You can discuss sensitive topics without deliberately targeting or abusing people.

Choose the appropriate topic for your recording so others can discover content they're interested in.

You don't have to be boring. Just be thoughtful.

4. Respect other people's voices and privacy

Only record and share content that you have the right to share.

  • Don't upload private conversations without appropriate permission.
  • Don't impersonate another person or misleadingly present their voice as your own.
  • Don't publish someone else's personal information without permission.
  • Don't share private voice messages, photographs or recordings from Connect without the other person's consent.
  • Don't use recordings to threaten, blackmail, embarrass or exploit someone.

A voice can be just as personal as a photograph. Treat it with the same respect.

5. Discover: Say something worth hearing

Discover is RÖST's open social space.

Share stories, jokes, music, opinions, poetry, observations or whatever else you've got on your mind.

You can be funny, serious, curious, passionate or completely ridiculous.

Just remember:

  • Keep recordings relevant to their chosen topic.
  • Respect other people's perspectives.
  • Don't deliberately flood topics with repetitive or misleading content.
  • Don't use Discover to harass or target individuals.
  • Don't post illegal or exploitative material.

The best conversations often start with people seeing the world differently.

6. Connect: People before pictures

Connect is RÖST's voice-first dating space.

The idea is simple: get to know someone through their personality and voice before deciding whether to reveal photographs.

Respect that process.

  • Be honest about who you are.
  • Respect someone's decision not to match, reply or reveal their photographs.
  • Never pressure anyone to share photographs, personal details or contact information.
  • Don't send unwanted sexual content or make repeated unwanted advances.
  • Don't share private conversations or revealed photographs outside RÖST without permission.
  • Accept rejection gracefully.

A match isn't an obligation. A conversation isn't a promise. And a reveal is always a mutual choice.

Good chemistry starts with mutual respect.

7. Create: Your voice, your responsibility

Create is where your voice can become something more.

You might record a voiceover, narrate a script, read a poem, create a character, deliver a personalised message or offer your voice for a commercial project.

Whether you're recording for fun or being paid for your work, you're responsible for what you create and deliver.

Make it yours

Only offer recordings and performances that you have the right to create, share and license.

Don't use copyrighted scripts, music, sound recordings or other protected material without the necessary rights or permission.

Don't imitate or clone another person's identifiable voice in a misleading or unauthorised way.

Be clear about what you're offering

If you're accepting a recording request, make sure you understand what's being asked of you.

Agree on the scope of the recording, intended use, delivery expectations and any payment or licensing terms before accepting a project.

Respect the agreement

If someone pays for a recording, deliver what you've agreed to provide.

If you're commissioning a recording, respect the creator's time, work and agreed terms.

Understand your rights

Creators retain ownership of their original recordings unless they expressly agree otherwise.

Selling a recording does not automatically transfer every copyright, commercial usage right or permission to reuse the creator's voice.

Any licence or transfer of rights should be clearly agreed between the parties.

Your voice has value. Treat it that way.

8. No exploitation or illegal content

RÖST is strictly for adults aged 18 and over.

We do not permit child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, trafficking, non-consensual sexual content, threats of violence, fraud or other unlawful activity.

Content that promotes or facilitates these activities is prohibited.

Serious violations may result in immediate account suspension or permanent removal and may be reported to relevant authorities where required or appropriate.

Please refer to our Child Safety Standards and CSAE Policy for more information.

9. Reporting and blocking

Not every conversation will be brilliant. Not everyone will get along. That's normal.

But if someone makes you uncomfortable, harasses you, breaks these guidelines or behaves inappropriately, you can use RÖST's reporting and blocking features.

You don't owe anyone a conversation, an explanation or a reply.

We review reports and may take action where content or behaviour violates our guidelines or Terms of Use.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include removing content, restricting account features, suspending accounts or permanently banning users.

We encourage people to report genuine concerns rather than engage in arguments or retaliation.

10. Help make RÖST worth listening to

RÖST is built around a simple idea: people have more to offer than a photograph, a profile or an endless scroll.

Every voice brings something different.

You don't need to be famous, beautiful, perfectly articulate or particularly interesting to everyone.

Just bring something of yourself.

Be curious. Be creative. Be respectful. Be human.

And above all, give people something worth listening to.
