# Terms of use: publication checklist

Lifted out of the draft so it cannot be published by accident.
Every item here is still open.

1. **Confirm the legal operator**: company name, registered address, jurisdiction, company number, and legal/safety/privacy contact channels.
2. **Match the product reality**: verify whether the three-voice-message rule is exactly three *each*, whether Connect separation is technically enforced, when photos can be viewed, whether screenshots are restricted, and how users can withdraw consent.
3. **Confirm launch features**: whether Rooms, Create requests, paid creator services and in-app calls actually exist. Remove future-feature language or clearly mark it as planned.
4. **Age assurance and safety**: verify the actual 18+ checks, report/block tools, appeal routes, moderation practices and UK Online Safety Act compliance work. Store age ratings alone may not meet all obligations.
5. **Verify the privacy-policy link** and ensure the Terms do not contradict the Privacy Policy on retention, audio processing, AI training, deletion or third-party vendors.
6. **Confirm payments**: if all features are free, say so unambiguously; if payments launch, add separate marketplace, consumer, creator and refund terms before accepting transactions.
7. **Legal review**: have UK-qualified counsel assess consumer fairness, platform moderation duties, privacy, governing law, IP licence and any dating/creator-specific risks.

**Drafting note:** This document adapts the supplied NaviSavi Terms of Use to RÖST. It intentionally does not carry over the original's Nebraska arbitration, one-year claim bar, sweeping indemnity, perpetual commercial licence to user content, or blanket exclusion of liability. Those provisions are not suitable to copy unquestioningly into UK consumer-facing terms.
