# Privacy policy: publication checklist

Lifted out of the draft so it cannot be published by accident.
Every item here is still open.

1. Identify the actual legal controller, registered address and privacy contact; assess UK/EU representative and DPO obligations.
2. Verify all statements about Discover/Connect separation, three-message gate, mutual reveal, original-photo access controls and message privacy against the deployed code and database policies.
3. Document every actual processor, SDK, storage location and international transfer safeguard.
4. Decide and document age assurance and any processing of selfies, face images or age-estimation outputs; review UK online-safety duties.
5. Determine lawful bases and additional Article 9 conditions for any sensitive dating preferences or inferred sensitive characteristics.
6. Confirm moderation access to private audio/photos, transcription, automated safety systems and the associated notices.
7. Add accurate retention periods, account deletion steps, backups and report retention rules.
8. Confirm whether Create involves payments, commissions, identity verification or creator payouts and add the appropriate disclosures if so.
9. Verify marketing consent, cookie practices, launch-list handling and whether any advertising SDKs are deployed.
10. Obtain UK privacy/legal review before publication. Do not copy the NaviSavi US state addendum without determining whether RÖST actually falls within the relevant US state laws.
