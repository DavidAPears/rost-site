What RÖST is

RÖST is a voice-first social platform. Three things sit behind one tab bar, and all of them are made of voices rather than pictures.

The argument is simple. A voice carries more of a person than a photograph does. You can hear whether somebody is funny, whether they are nervous, where they grew up, whether they mean it. None of that survives a grid of images.

Discover

See Discover. A social feed you listen to. Stories, opinions, jokes and conversations, up to thirty seconds each, tagged with what they are about.

Rooms live here too: places to talk about one thing, or to listen to somebody who knows about it.

Connect

See Connect. Meet someone through their voice and personality, with the pictures turned off. You answer a few questions out loud, you hear other people answer theirs, and nobody sees a face until you have both said yes to that.

Connect is kept deliberately separate from the rest of the app. A Connect name, the answers you record there and any photos you later share are never attached to a public profile.

Create

See Create. Your own page, and a way to be hired from it. Say what you are up for recording, get discovered by the people who hear you, take requests and get paid.

It is free while RÖST is new, and nothing charges anybody today.

What it is instead of

RÖST is an alternative to the apps people already spend their evenings in, the messaging apps, the photo feeds and the dating apps. It is not a podcast platform and it is not trying to be one. Thirty seconds of somebody talking is not a show, it is a person.

Safety

RÖST is for people aged 18 and over, everywhere, with no exceptions.

Anything on RÖST can be reported from inside the app: a recording, a message, a profile, a room or a person. Reports are reviewed, and what is done about them is recorded with a written reason, so that a moderation decision is something that can be reviewed rather than something that simply happened.

Our approach to child sexual abuse and exploitation is set out in full in our CSAE policy. How we handle personal information is in our privacy policy, and the rules for using RÖST are in our terms.

Common questions

What is RÖST?

RÖST is a voice-first social platform. Three things sit behind one tab bar and all of them are made of voices rather than pictures: Discover, a social feed you listen to; Connect, a way to meet people through conversation before photographs; and Create, a page you can be hired from for voice work.

Is RÖST a dating app?

Connect is, and it works voice first: you answer a few questions out loud, you hear other people answer theirs, and nobody sees a face until you have both said yes to that. Photographs stay locked until three messages in. The rest of RÖST is not a dating app. Discover is a social feed and Create is for voice work, and your Connect identity is kept deliberately separate from both.

How long is a voice note on RÖST?

A post on Discover is up to thirty seconds, which is long enough to say something and short enough that nobody is committing an evening. A freestyle piece can run to a minute, a private voice message to a minute, and a recording somebody has asked a creator for to three minutes.

Do I have to show my face?

No. Discover is audio, and a profile picture is optional. On Connect, photographs are locked until three messages in, and until then no photograph is sent to the other person’s device at all.

Is RÖST free?

Yes. It is free while RÖST is new, and nothing charges anybody today.

How old do you have to be to use RÖST?

Eighteen and over, everywhere, with no exceptions.

Can I find people near me?

Yes. You can choose an area to show on your profile and set how far away you are willing to look, so Discover and Connect can surface people around you as well as people anywhere.

Can I get voice work on RÖST?

That is what Create is for. You make a page with a bio and voice samples, say what you are up for recording, and people who hear you can find the page and ask. Commercial, narration, character work, audiobooks, podcasts and user-generated content are the kinds of thing people ask for.

Is RÖST a podcast app?

No, and it is not trying to be one. Thirty seconds of somebody talking is not a show, it is a person. RÖST is an alternative to the apps people already spend their evenings in: the messaging apps, the photo feeds and the dating apps.

How do I delete my RÖST account?

From inside the app, or from rost.fyi/delete if you cannot get into your account. Deleting removes the account and everything attached to it. Reports and moderation records are kept, because otherwise deleting your account would be a way to destroy the complaints about you.
