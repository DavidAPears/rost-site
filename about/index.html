What RÖST is

RÖST is a voice-first social platform, launching in London. Three things sit behind one tab bar, and all of them are made of voices rather than pictures.

The argument is simple. A voice carries more of a person than a photograph does. You can hear whether somebody is funny, whether they are nervous, where they grew up, whether they mean it. None of that survives a grid of images.

Discover

A social feed you listen to. Stories, opinions, jokes and conversations, up to thirty seconds each, tagged with what they are about.

Rooms live here too: places to talk about one thing, or to listen to somebody who knows about it.

Connect

Meet someone through their voice and personality, with the pictures turned off. You answer a few questions out loud, you hear other people answer theirs, and nobody sees a face until you have both said yes to that.

Connect is kept deliberately separate from the rest of the app. A Connect name, the answers you record there and any photos you later share are never attached to a public profile.

Create

Your own page, and a way to be hired from it. Say what you are up for recording, get discovered by the people who hear you, and take requests.

It is free while RÖST is new, and nothing charges anybody today.

What it is instead of

RÖST is an alternative to the apps people already spend their evenings in, the messaging apps, the photo feeds and the dating apps. It is not a podcast platform and it is not trying to be one. Thirty seconds of somebody talking is not a show, it is a person.

Safety

RÖST is for people aged 18 and over, everywhere, with no exceptions.

Anything on RÖST can be reported from inside the app: a recording, a message, a profile, a room or a person. Reports are reviewed, and what is done about them is recorded with a written reason, so that a moderation decision is something that can be reviewed rather than something that simply happened.

Our approach to child sexual abuse and exploitation is set out in full in our CSAE policy. How we handle personal information is in our privacy policy, and the rules for using RÖST are in our terms.

Where we are now

RÖST is not open yet. It is launching in London first, and the people who leave an email on the home page hear about it before anybody else.
