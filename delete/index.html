Delete your account

PermanentNo undo

Deleting your RÖST account removes it and the things you made with it. It happens immediately, it cannot be undone, and we cannot bring an account back once it is gone.

You do not need to be signed in to read this page. You do need to sign in to delete an account, because the only account we will ever delete is the one you are signed in to.

What is deleted

  • Your account and the email address it signs in with.
  • Your profile, your display name and your avatar.
  • Every voice recording you made, in Discover, Connect and Create.
  • Your Connect profile, your answers, your matches and your identity photos.
  • Your voice messages, your follows, your saves and your settings.

What does not go, and why

Two things deliberately survive. We would rather tell you now than have you find out afterwards.

  • Reports made about you stay, without your name on them. The report, the reason and what the reporter wrote are kept; the link to your account is removed. If a report about somebody disappeared the moment they deleted themselves, deleting your account would be a way of destroying evidence.
  • Rooms you started may carry on without you. A room with another moderator passes to whoever has been a moderator longest, because a room with members is somebody else's too. A room nobody else is running goes with your account.

Anything other people already heard, saved or recorded on another device is outside our reach. We cannot delete somebody else's memory of a conversation.

How long the rest is kept

Everything in the first list goes when you press the button. It is not held for a cooling off period and there is no window in which you can change your mind.

Copies may sit in backups for a short time before those are overwritten on their own schedule. Reports and the record of any moderation decision are kept for as long as they may be needed to keep people safe or to meet a legal obligation, and no longer. Our privacy policy sets out the detail.

If you were on the launch list rather than a member, write to us and we will take that address off.
